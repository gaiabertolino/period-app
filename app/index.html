    Import CSV

    One row per period start, header cycle_start_date, dates as YYYY-MM-DD. Extra columns are kept.

    Back up (export CSV)

    iOS may delete website data that hasn't been used for a while. Export a backup regularly.

    Calendar reminders

    Creates an .ics file with the next predicted dates as all-day events, with the alerts chosen in Settings. Without a server the app can't move these alerts on its own: after you log a new date, export again and re-import the file.

    Delete everything

    Removes all dates and settings from this device. This can't be undone.

    Shown on the lock screen. Keep it discreet.

    Calendar alerts

    Comma-separated, 0 = on the day. Leave empty for no alerts. Events always cover the whole day.

    When you save, edit or delete a date, the app opens this Shortcut. It replaces the predictions in your "Cycle" calendar and adds the alerts to your "Cycle" reminders list. Set the Shortcut up once by following these steps.

    Cycle lengths used by the model (days)

    Gaps outside this range are probably logging errors or skipped months. They stay in your history but don't count toward the prediction.

    Advanced: prior

    Normal-Inverse-Gamma prior on cycle length. The defaults assume about 28 ± 4 days and give way to your own data after a few cycles.

    About

    Not a medical device. Not a contraceptive method. These predictions are statistical estimates and must not be used for medical or contraceptive decisions. If you are worried about your cycle, talk to a health professional.

    Privacy. There is no server and no account. Your dates are stored only in this browser's storage (IndexedDB) on this device, and the app makes no network requests with your data. Files leave the device only when you export them yourself.

    Open source (MIT). The model is a Bayesian Normal-Inverse-Gamma fit with a Student-t predictive.
