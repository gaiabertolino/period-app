# Disclaimer

**This app is not a medical device.** It has not been evaluated, certified or
approved by any regulator, and it does not diagnose, treat or monitor any
condition.

**This app is not a contraceptive method.** Do not use its predictions to
avoid or plan a pregnancy. Ovulation and fertile days are not predicted, and
predicting the next period start says nothing reliable about them.

**Do not base clinical decisions on these predictions.** They are statistical
estimates from the dates you entered, under a simple model that assumes your
cycle lengths are roughly stable over time. They can be wrong, especially with
few data, irregular cycles, after pregnancy or breastfeeding, when starting or
stopping hormonal medication, around menopause, or if dates were logged
incorrectly. A credible interval at 80% means the real date is expected to fall
outside it about one time in five.

If your cycle changes noticeably, stops, or you have pain, very heavy bleeding
or other symptoms, talk to a doctor or another qualified health professional.

**Data and privacy.** All data is stored only on your device, in your
browser's storage. The authors never receive it and cannot recover it. You are
responsible for your own backups (Data → Download CSV). Browsers, iOS in
particular, can delete website data, for example after a long period of
non-use or when the device is low on storage.
